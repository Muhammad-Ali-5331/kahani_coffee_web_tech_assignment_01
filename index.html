Why regulars keep coming back

Small details, brewed on purpose

Beans with a passport

Single-origin lots from small farms, roasted in-house every Tuesday so the cup you drink was roasted days, not months, ago.

Baked before sunrise

Our kitchen opens at 5am. Croissants, cardamom buns and biscotti come out warm, well before the first pour.

A room built for lingering

Big windows, quiet corners, and staff who remember your usual order after the second visit.
