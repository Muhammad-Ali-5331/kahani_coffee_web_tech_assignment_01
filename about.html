Our story

Started as a home roaster, grew into a neighbourhood room.

Kahani began in 2019 as a weekend hobby — a hand-crank roaster on a balcony and a handful of curious neighbours. Five years on, it's a working coffeehouse, but the questions we ask of every bean and every guest haven't changed: where is this from, and what brought you in today?

How we got here

A short timeline

The balcony roaster

A second-hand drum roaster and green beans bought in 5kg bags — the first "batches" filled the whole street with smoke.

The first counter

A 200 sq. ft. shopfront on MM Alam Road, four tables, and a menu written in chalk that changed weekly.

The kitchen opens

Bakes joined the counter, starting with the cardamom bun that is still our best seller.

Kahani today

A full coffeehouse with in-house roasting, a bakery, and regulars who've become friends.

What we stand for

Values that shape every cup

Traceable sourcing

Every bag names the farm and the harvest year. If we can't trace it, we don't buy it.

Slow by design

We don't rush pour-overs to hit a queue target. A good cup takes the time it takes.

A seat for everyone

Students, freelancers, families — the room is built to welcome all of them, all day.

Honest pricing

No surge pricing, no hidden service charge. The price on the menu is the price you pay.
